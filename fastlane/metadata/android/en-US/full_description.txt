An open-source EEG application for consumer headsets. It streams your signal,
stores it on your device, and helps you see how your current state compares with
your own normal.

The reference is you. Not a population average, not a benchmark, not a score out
of a hundred. The app learns what your ordinary looks like and shows you when you
have moved away from it.

WHAT IT DOES
• Streams 12-channel EEG from the Master and Dynamic MW75 Neuro over Bluetooth.
• Parses and stores every reading locally, in a database on your phone.
• Estimates a state from your own recent history, so the comparison is against
  yourself.
• Keeps streaming while the screen is off, through a foreground service you can
  see and stop.

WHAT IT REFUSES TO DO
• Nothing is uploaded. There is no cloud sync, no account and no server of ours.
• No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.
• No score, no streak, no leaderboard. Your brain is not a game.
• It does not tell you what to do. It shows you what changed and leaves the
  reading to you.

OPEN, AND HONEST ABOUT ITS LIMITS
Ebbflow is open source, including the device layer and the packet parser, which
began as a port of the reference Python streamer. The state estimate is a research
instrument built around a single-subject study, not a validated clinical measure.

You need a compatible EEG headset. The app is the software half only.

Ebbflow is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any
condition, and it is not for clinical decisions. If you want to share a recording
with your own clinician, that is an export you perform deliberately.
